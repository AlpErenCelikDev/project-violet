Building a World From Scratch

September 4, 2026

The hardest part of making a game is usually not what ends up on screen, but the world built behind it. For Project Violet, we're starting by nailing down the rules, the atmosphere, and the balance between darkness and magic that runs through everything.

Every decision we make along the way will be shared here in future posts. For now, all we can say is: there's a long road ahead, and it's an exciting one.
