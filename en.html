GET READY FOR THE ADVENTURE!

Project Violet is an indie RPG project set in a dark world, combining story and sandbox experiences.

You can choose a profession, build your own order, trade, explore new regions, and become a part of events that shape the world's future.

However, we won't be revealing all the secrets of this world just yet.

You'll have to discover some things for yourself.

A LIVING WORLD

In Project Violet, the world doesn't just wait for the player.

Cities, NPCs, trade, and political events move according to their own rules.

Major changes in the story will be able to impact the world itself.

A decision might do more than just change a quest.

ECONOMY: NOT JUST MONEY

In the world of Project Violet, not everyone knows everything.

Some information can be more valuable for certain players.

What is the true value of an item?

Where should it be sold?

Today, or tomorrow?

Sometimes the most valuable thing isn't the gold in your pocket, but something you know.

WHEN THE STORY ENDS, THE WORLD DOES NOT

Project Violet's story will have an ending.

But the world doesn't have to end.

After the events of the story unfold, cities, relationships, economy, and the lives of players will continue on their own paths.

Perhaps the real story will begin right then.

UNDER DEVELOPMENT

Project Violet is currently at the very beginning of the road, and this is a very long journey.
