Frequently Asked Questions

Here's what people most often ask about Project Violet.

What is Project Violet?

Project Violet is an independent game project that combines a story, RPG mechanics, and sandbox systems.

Players can choose their own professions, explore the world, and become a part of the unfolding events.

When will the game be released?

There is no exact release date yet.

Project Violet is in early development, and our priority is to build the core systems of the game solidly.

What platforms will it be released on?

The game is primarily being developed for PC.

As the development process progresses, official announcements will be made through this site.

How many different professions will there be?

There will be multiple professions, and each one will offer a different gameplay style.

Roles currently being worked on include mining, trading, blacksmithing, farming, and combat-focused paths.

Will Project Violet be multiplayer?

Multiplayer features are among the project ideas.

However, the scope of the multiplayer system and at what stage of the game it will be implemented have not been finalized yet.

Will the story make up the entirety of the game?

No.

Alongside the story aspect, Project Violet also features sandbox systems.

The world is intended to continue living even after the story comes to an end.

What will the game's graphics look like?

Project Violet is being developed in a pixel art style.

Our goal is to create a unique, atmospheric, and cohesive visual world.

Who is developing the game?

Project Violet is being developed as an independent game project.

Throughout the development process, we will share how the project grows and what we learn via the Dev Blog.

Is there a trailer or demo?

Not yet.

Our first goal is to build the playable core systems.

Once the first serious playable contents are ready, they will be shared here.
